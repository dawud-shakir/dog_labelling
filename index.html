Labelling guide
  • walk: any forward locomotion, including trot and run.
  • sit: rump on the ground, forelegs extended.
  • lie: chest or side on the ground.
  • stand: on all four legs, not travelling (turning in place, sniffing, tail-wagging count as stand).
  • other / mixed: a clear posture change inside the clip, a jump, being carried, or not one of the four.
  • unclear: dog too small, cut off, or blurred to judge.
